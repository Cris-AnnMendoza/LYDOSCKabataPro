Who We Are

About the Program

The Local Youth Development System of Sta. Cruz, Laguna is a government-backed initiative dedicated to nurturing the potential of every young Filipino in our community.

Our Mission

To empower the youth of Sta. Cruz through accessible programs, leadership training, and community-driven opportunities that build character and competence.

Our Vision

A thriving, engaged, and empowered youth sector that actively contributes to the social, economic, and cultural development of Sta. Cruz, Laguna.

Our Values

Integrity, inclusivity, service, and excellence — guiding every program and initiative we deliver to the youth of our community.

Data Privacy Policy

Republic Act No. 10173 – Data Privacy Act of 2012

Purpose of Data Collection

The Local Youth Development Office (LYDO) of Sta. Cruz, Laguna collects your personal information solely for the purpose of youth registration, program enrollment, event participation, and delivery of government services under the Local Youth Development Program.

Data We Collect

  • Personal details (name, birthdate, gender, civil status)
  • Contact information (email, phone number, address)
  • Educational and employment information
  • Youth classification and organizational affiliation
  • Skills, interests, and program preferences

How We Protect Your Data

Your data is stored securely in our system and protected through encryption and access controls. Only authorized LYDO personnel may access your information. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties without your consent, except as required by law.

Your Rights Under RA 10173

  • Right to be informed — You have the right to know how your data is used.
  • Right to access — You may request a copy of your personal data on file.
  • Right to correction — You may request corrections to inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure — You may request deletion of your data subject to legal limitations.
  • Right to object — You may object to the processing of your personal data.

Data Retention

Your personal data will be retained for the duration of your registration with the LYDO and for a period required by applicable laws and regulations. Data will be securely disposed of once no longer needed.

Contact the Data Protection Officer

For concerns regarding your personal data, you may contact the LYDO Data Protection Officer at:
Email: lydosantacruzlaguna@gmail.com
Address: Municipal Hall (Old Building), Cailles Street, Poblacion III, Santa Cruz, Laguna, Philippines 4009

This policy is in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations issued by the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines.
